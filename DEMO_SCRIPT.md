## Word-For-Word Recording Script

### [0:00 – 0:30] 1. The Product & Instant 5-Second Triage

> **"Hi everyone, I'm excited to present LeadPulse AI, an intelligent lead qualification and sales copilot built specifically for high-velocity real estate brokerages and AI voice teams.**
>
> In real estate, the first broker to respond with tailored context wins the deal. We designed this interface with an AWS-grade enterprise aesthetic—clean, dense, and scannable in under 5 seconds.
>
> On the left, our triage feed highlights priority tiers at a glance: **Hot**, **Warm**, and **Nurture**, sorted dynamically by AI qualification score, budget, or recency."

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] 2. The Main Workflow End-to-End
*(Visual: Click the orange `+ Intake Lead` button. Show the modal. Highlight the Document Upload zone and Voice Dictation button. Click "Load Sample Lead" or upload a file, then click "Analyze & Score Lead with AI".)*

> **"Here is our core qualification workflow.**
>
> In real operations, leads don't arrive cleanly—they come as raw emails, PDF property inquiries, or voicemail transcripts. Our intake engine supports multi-format drag-and-drop ingestion—PDF, CSV, text files—as well as browser-native microphone dictation.
>
> When we submit a lead, our AI analysis pipeline extracts key signals:
> 1. A calibrated **Lead Score (0 to 100)** and Priority Tier.
> 2. An **Executive Summary** and underlying **Customer Intent**.
> 3. Extracted **Key Specs** and hidden **Objections**.
> 4. An authoritative **Recommended Next Action**, and a personalized, 1-click **Suggested Response** that the agent can dispatch immediately."

---

### [1:15 – 1:55] 3. Added Feature: Deal Accelerator Suite™ & Live Voice Agent Simulator
*(Visual: Click the `Deal Accelerator Suite™` tab on Marcus Vance's lead. Scroll through the Live Phone Battlecard and Smart Inventory Matcher. Then click `Live Voice Demo` in the top header and start a live call.)*

> **"Beyond standard qualification, I invented the Deal Accelerator Suite™ to turn cold data into closed deals:**
>
> 1. **Live Phone Battlecards**: Before picking up the phone, an agent gets a 7-second opening hook and tactical rebuttals to the buyer's exact hesitations.
> 2. **Smart Inventory Matcher**: The system automatically cross-references our active listings, calculating a budget and requirement fit score with instant 1-click WhatsApp and Email drafting.
> 3. **Live Voice Agent Simulator**: As an FDE sitting between clients and technology, I built a live browser-based voice call simulator with real-time speech audio and visual waveforms, allowing sales leaders to audition how our autonomous voice agent qualifies prospects on a live call."

---

### [1:55 – 2:30] 4. One Interesting Technical & Product Decision
*(Visual: Click `Explain Score (Audit)` to reveal the Buyer Trust modal. Then click `Voice Config` to reveal the FDE tuning drawer.)*

> **"One key technical and product decision I'd like to highlight is our 'Grounded Explainability & Configurable Buyer Trust Model'.**
>
> Many AI sales tools present arbitrary numbers that non-technical buyers don't trust. Instead of a black-box LLM score, we implemented a deterministic factor attribution breakdown:
> - Budget liquidity accounts for 35% of the score.
> - Timeline urgency accounts for 30%.
> - Scope clarity accounts for 20%.
> - Objection risk accounts for 15%.
>
> When a client asks on a call, *'Why did your AI label this buyer Hot?'*, the FDE can immediately pull up this plain-English audit report. Furthermore, our live **Voice Config Drawer** allows us to tune voice latency, interruption sensitivity, and seller aggressiveness live on the call, proving to buyers that the system is fully controllable and production-ready."

## 3 Quick Tips for Recording
1. **Tool**: Use [Loom.com](https://www.loom.com) (free, browser extension) or **OBS Studio** / Windows Game Bar (<kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>).
2. **Resolution**: Full-screen browser window at 1080p, zoom set to 100%.
3. **Pacing**: Speak smoothly; following the script above takes approximately **2 minutes and 20 seconds**, well within the 3-minute hard ceiling.
